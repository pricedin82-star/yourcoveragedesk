Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

This policy explains what information Your Coverage Desk ("we", "us") collects, why, and what we do with it. It covers this website, our social media accounts, our newsletter, and the internal publishing software we operate.

Information we collect

  • Newsletter email addresses. If you subscribe, we collect the email address you give us and the date you subscribed. Nothing else is required.
  • Purchase information. If you buy a digital product, our payment processor collects and processes your payment details. We never see or store full card numbers.
  • Aggregate platform statistics. Instagram, TikTok and Facebook give us anonymous, aggregated figures about our own posts, such as view counts and follower totals. These are not tied to individuals by us.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect, request or store: policy numbers, claim numbers, government identification numbers, insurance documents, medical records, or details of anyone's specific insurance claim. If you send information of this kind to us, we will not act on it and we will delete it.

Our publishing software

We operate software that publishes our own original videos and images to our own social media accounts. It authenticates only to accounts we own, using each platform's official API. It does not sign in on behalf of any other person, does not read other users' content or personal data, and is not offered to any third party.

How we use information

Email addresses are used to send the newsletter and to deliver products you have purchased. Aggregate statistics are used to decide what to publish next. That is all.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We share information only with the service providers that make the project run — our email platform, our payment processor and our hosting provider — and only to the extent they need it to provide that service.

Retention

Email addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Every newsletter contains an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes your address.

Your rights

You may ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to the contact address below and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

This project is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
