About Your Coverage Desk

Educational content about how U.S. auto insurance coverage actually works.

You bought a price, not a policy. Most drivers pick a premium, not a set of coverages — and they find out the difference at the worst possible moment.

Your Coverage Desk explains what each line of an auto policy actually means, in plain English, from the perspective of a licensed claims adjuster: bodily injury and property damage liability, PIP and medical payments, comprehensive and collision, deductibles, rental reimbursement, uninsured and underinsured motorist coverage, and how commercial auto differs from personal auto.

What this site is

This site is the home of an educational publishing project. Short videos and written explainers are published to Instagram under the name Your Coverage Desk, and to an email newsletter.

What this is not

  • It is not legal advice or insurance advice.
  • It does not sell, quote, place or broker insurance of any kind.
  • It does not represent claimants, adjust claims for the public, or give guidance on any specific claim.
  • It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or speaking for any insurance carrier or employer.

Coverage terms vary by policy, endorsement, carrier and state. Read your own policy and talk to your agent or carrier about your specific situation.
