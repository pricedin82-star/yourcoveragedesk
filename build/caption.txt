"Full coverage" isn't a product. It's a phrase people use for liability + comprehensive + collision — and it leaves out the coverages that matter most when something goes wrong.

Pull up your declarations page. If a coverage isn't listed with a limit next to it, you don't have it.

Save this for the next time someone tells you they're "fully covered."
